The funniest excuse a robot vacuum could give, in a single line: it
blames the missing sock on a top-secret mission under the couch, and
the cat for sitting on its power button mid-mission.

## Entry

> I didn't eat your sock. I was on a top-secret mission under the couch when the cat sat on my power button.

The line is 106 characters, within the 140-character cap.

## How it works

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Standard library only, no network access.
